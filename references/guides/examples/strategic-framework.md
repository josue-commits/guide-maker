## 🎯 The Automation Framework

Most sales teams automate the wrong things first. They start with email sequences and ignore the 10 hours a week they spend on data entry, lead research, and CRM updates.

The rule is simple: automate the boring stuff first, the customer-facing stuff second. Here's why. If your AI sends a bad email, a prospect notices. If your AI enters data wrong, only your CRM notices (and it's easier to fix).

## Step 1: Automate Your CRM Data Entry

**Goal:** Stop manually entering contact info, deal stages, and call notes.

**Time saved:** 5-8 hours per week for a team of 3.

Every call, every email, every LinkedIn message should auto-log to your CRM without anyone touching it. The setup takes about an hour with most modern CRM platforms.

What to automate first:
- Contact creation from inbound forms (zero manual entry)
- Deal stage updates based on email replies or meeting completions
- Call notes transcribed and attached to the contact record automatically
- LinkedIn connection requests logged as touchpoints

What to leave manual (for now):
- Deal value estimates (too much context needed)
- Win/loss reasons (your judgment matters here)
- Strategic account notes (AI misses nuance)

> 💡 **Pro Tip:** The test for whether something should be automated: if a new hire could do it with a checklist and no judgment calls, automate it.

---

## Step 2: Set Up Lead Enrichment

**Goal:** Know who your leads are before you ever talk to them.

**Time saved:** 2-3 hours per week.

When a new lead enters your pipeline, an enrichment workflow should fire automatically and pull:

- Company size and revenue range
- Tech stack (what tools they already use)
- Recent funding or hiring signals
- Decision-maker contact info
- Industry and business model

The output should land in your CRM as structured fields, not a wall of text. That way you can filter and sort by company size, industry, or tech stack when planning your outreach.

## 📋 Quick Reference

| Stage | What to Automate | Tools | Time to Set Up | Weekly Time Saved |
|-------|-----------------|-------|----------------|-------------------|
| Data Layer | CRM entry, logging | Zapier, native integrations | 1-2 hours | 5-8 hours |
| Research Layer | Enrichment, signals | Clay, Apollo, Clearbit | 2-3 hours | 2-3 hours |
| Communication | Follow-ups, scheduling | AI email tools, Calendly | 3-4 hours | 4-6 hours |
| Reporting | Pipeline reports | CRM native, Notion | 1 hour | 1-2 hours |
| Scoring | Lead qualification | CRM scoring, custom rules | 2 hours | 1-2 hours |

Start with Stage 1. Get that running clean for two weeks before moving to Stage 2. The most common mistake is trying to automate everything at once, getting overwhelmed, and going back to doing it all manually.
